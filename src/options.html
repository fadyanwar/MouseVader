    How MouseVader works, and its limits

    Many sites record where your pointer goes to learn what interests you. MouseVader adds fake pointer visits to random elements on the page: it enters an element, moves around inside it for a moment and leaves, the way a mouse does. Trackers record those visits next to your real ones, which makes the profile they build of you less accurate.

    It never clicks, types, scrolls or touches form fields, video or editable areas, and it pauses while you use the page so it doesn't close menus you opened. Browsers mark script-generated events as untrusted (isTrusted = false); a tracker that checks this flag can filter MouseVader's events out. Many don't, but it's a limit no extension can remove.
